What it does.

Every screen follows the same clock, one person is in control at a time, and changes wait until you press Apply. If something you need is missing, say so.

Displays, confidence monitors and operators' phones open a link in a browser, with nothing to install on each machine. The links work from anywhere with an internet connection.

Works with Companion

A native Companion module with a start/pause toggle, nudges, set duration, running order Prev / Take / Next, and live time and colour on the keys. It connects over the internet, from wherever Companion runs.It connects to the laptop over your show network.

Whoever's best placed runs it

The switcher op backstage, the graphics op, the show caller at FOH or a dedicated operator: anyone with the control link can run the clock. Everyone else is in observer mode until they tap Take over, and every panel shows who's in control.

Run it from your phone

The live controls fit on one phone screen: timer, Start/Pause, Reset, ±1 and ±5. Changes wait as a draft until you Apply, so nothing reaches the screen by accident.

Real clocks as well as screens

A bridge drives CDEther / Hive LED displays from the same timer, so the lectern clock and the stage screen always agree.

Every breakout on one dashboard

Run all your rooms side by side. Each room has its own control and display links, made from long random codes, so nobody can reach your controls by guessing an address. A display link only ever shows the timer.

Talk to the stage, quietly

Send "Wrap up" or "Take questions" to the speaker, full screen or as a ticker, without touching the countdown. The display also shows who's on and who's next from your running order.

Operate from anywhere

For hybrid events and remote crews: the control link works on any internet connection, so the person running the clock doesn't have to be in the room.

Screens plugged into the laptop

Each monitor or projector connected to the laptop can show a room, full screen, chosen from the dashboard. Two extra screens can show two different rooms.

The details.

Everything the display can show, and how you run it.

On the display
The countdown, speaker or session name, "Up next", the time now ("Now 14:32"), the finish time ("Finish 14:55"), status and room label. Switch any of them off, down to digits only.
Duration or End at
Count down a length of time, or count to a clock time ("finish at 14:30"). The server keeps the time, so every screen agrees.
Count modes
Count down and stop at 0:00, carry on counting the overrun, or count up as a stopwatch. H:MM:SS past an hour.
Clock mode
Switch the display to a time-of-day clock for breaks and changeovers.
Warnings
Green, amber, red, then purple on overrun, flashing at zero. You set the thresholds and colours per room, and can use them when counting up too.
Messages
Send "Wrap up" or "Take questions" to the stage, full screen or as a ticker. Clear it with one tap.
Background
Black, any colour, or your own image: fill the screen, fit it, or stretch it. Plain green, blue or black gives your vision mixer a clean picture to key.
Size & fit
Set the size and font of the digits, from standard or condensed to LED segments, with or without a glow. The timer fits to width on any screen, portrait or landscape, and goes full screen with one click.
Running order
Load your speakers and session lengths, or paste in your run of show as text, then step through with Prev / Take / Next.
Quick presets
Eight one-tap durations. Ctrl+click to save your own.
Keyboard
Space to start or pause, R to reset, +/- to nudge, and arrows and Enter to work through the running order.
Survives restarts
Rooms and timer state are saved, so a server restart picks up where it left off.

You'll need

An internet connection on every device. If the venue's internet can't be trusted, the Windows version runs without it.

  1. Download and installOn Windows 10 or 11. Windows may show a SmartScreen warning because the installer isn't code-signed yet: choose More info, then Run anyway.
  2. Open it once with internetThat starts your free 14-day trial. After that it doesn't need the internet.
  3. Plug in your screensEach monitor or projector on the laptop can show a room, chosen from the dashboard.
  4. Join phones and displaysThey scan a QR code from the dashboard, on the same network as the laptop.
