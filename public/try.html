Things to try

  1. Press Start and watch the display turn amber at 1:00 and red at 0:30.
  2. Step through the programme with Next and Take. The speaker and "up next" lines change on the display.
  3. Send a message to the stage, full screen or as a ticker, then clear it.
  4. Open the control link on a second device. It starts in observer mode until you tap Take over.
  5. Switch to End at and count down to a clock time instead.
